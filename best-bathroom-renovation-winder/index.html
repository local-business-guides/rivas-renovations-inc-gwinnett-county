Checks to make before choosing

Use this short list when you speak with a prospective contractor:

  • Can they explain the proposed scope in everyday terms?
  • Do they ask about how your household uses the room?
  • Are the project steps and communication expectations discussed?
  • Do you know which decisions are yours to make and when they are needed?
  • Are unclear items identified rather than left for you to assume?

These questions help you see whether you are aligned on the work. Make notes during each conversation so you can compare answers later. If the project involves several changes, ask how those pieces fit together and which details need further review before work is planned.

Watch for avoidable selection mistakes

Do not choose based on a slogan or a single idea about how the finished room should look. A visually appealing plan may miss the practical issue that prompted the renovation. Also, do not assume that a broad remodeling description automatically covers every task you have in mind. Confirm the scope directly, including what has not yet been decided.
