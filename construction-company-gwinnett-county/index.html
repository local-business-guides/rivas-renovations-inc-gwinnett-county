## Questions to ask before work starts
A conversation before hiring can help you understand the proposed scope and what you need to decide. Bring a list of questions, and ask for explanations in plain language. You can ask:

  • Which parts of the home are included in the work?
  • What decisions or materials need to be settled before the job begins?
  • Could the project affect nearby rooms, outdoor areas, or access to the house?
  • How will updates be shared if the scope or sequence needs attention?
  • What should be moved or protected before the work gets underway?

The answers can help you compare options without relying on assumptions. A construction company Gwinnett County residents are considering should be able to discuss the intended work and the steps involved in a way you can follow.

## Get your home and information ready
Before contacting a contractor, note the areas that need attention and any related concerns. Photos can help explain damage or show how a room is used. If you have a preferred layout, examples of finishes, or a list of must-haves, keep those together. It also helps to identify practical limits, such as rooms that need to remain usable or times when workers should avoid a particular area.

For a larger project, separate essential changes from items you might consider later.
